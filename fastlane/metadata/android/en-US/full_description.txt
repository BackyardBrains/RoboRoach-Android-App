The BYB Backpack is a small, portable two-channel Bluetooth signal generator for safe, general-purpose microstimulation in K-12 classrooms. This app is the remote.

Tap Find Signal Generator to connect to the nearest backpack. Swipe left or right to send a pulse train on either output. In Settings you choose the signal: frequency, pulse width, duration, gain, and a random mode.

The backpack and this app were developed with funding from the National Institutes of Health (NIH).

Connect the backpack through the standard 0.1" header. You need the BYB Backpack hardware from Backyard Brains to use this app.

BYB Backpack uses Bluetooth. On Android 11 and older it also needs Location turned on so your phone can find nearby Bluetooth devices. The app does not collect or share any personal data.
